# Contributing

Submit changes through pull requests for maintainer review. Public visibility does not grant write access.

For ruling contributions, include:
- The question and complete relevant game state.
- Format, region, and effective date.
- Card identifiers and the applicable text version.
- Primary source URLs and retrieval dates.
- Whether the conclusion is explicitly published, inferred, or unresolved.
- A regression scenario with an expected answer and supporting evidence.

Do not invent citations or present an inferred interaction as an official ruling.
Do not commit credentials or third-party datasets without checking their reuse terms.

Branch protection and required-review settings must be configured separately in GitHub; this document does not enforce them.
